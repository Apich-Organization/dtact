## 2024-02-13 - [Optimize redundant loads in scheduler mailbox polling]
**Learning:** Found a performance optimization opportunity in `DtaScheduler::poll_mailboxes` and `route_chunk`. The `local_head` atomic was previously loaded in `poll_mailboxes` and passed implicitly by recalculating the queue length inside `route_chunk` which loaded `local_head` again. Hoisting this atomic load and passing the cached value downward into `route_chunk` eliminates repeated atomic read operations without bypassing safety limits, saving cycles during high frequency loops.
**Action:** Use cached atomic variables to avoid redundant atomic loads in hot paths, when the value cannot change on the current thread and when the variable is passed down rather than completely recalculating dependent states.
