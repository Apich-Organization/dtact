## 2025-02-18 - Optimized Atomic State Passing in DtaScheduler Iterative Queue Drain

**Learning:** In Dtact's `DtaScheduler`, `poll_mailboxes` and `drain_warehouse` iteratively poll chunks and route them to a local queue. Originally, they did an atomic load of `local_tail` every loop iteration and inside `route_chunk` to calculate the queue space. Since the worker thread itself is the *only* mutator of `local_tail`, the thread can maintain a locally-computed `cur_len` integer. By passing this integer down into helper functions (`route_chunk`) and returning the *delta* of tasks added, the redundant, high-frequency atomic reads of `local_tail` are completely eliminated in the hot path.
**Action:** When a loop modifies an atomic state variable but is the *only* writer to that state, hoist the initial read out of the loop and track the state using a local scalar variable. Pass this locally tracked state to helpers instead of having them re-read the atomic variable. Helpers should return their structural modifications (deltas) so the caller can locally accumulate the updates.
